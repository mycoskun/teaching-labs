Model and numbers

ML² θ̈ = Tc − MgL sin θ   linearized about θ̄: T ≈ MgL [sin θ̄ + cos θ̄ (θ − θ̄)]

Deviation error in the top plot: |ΔT − ΔTlin| / |ΔT| with ΔT = T − T̄. At θ̄ = 0 this is (θ − sin θ)/sin θ: 4.7 % at 30°, 5 % at 30.8°, 11.1 % at 45°. At θ̄ = 90° the slope is zero and the linear model keeps no deviation at all.

The time response is integrated with fourth-order Runge–Kutta (step 2 ms). The theoretical period is T/T₀ = 2K(k)/π with k = sin(θ₀/2), K the complete elliptic integral of the first kind (computed here with the arithmetic-geometric mean): 1.0174 at 30°, 1.0732 at 60°, 1.1803 at 90°. With L = 1 m and g = 9.81 m/s², T₀ = 2.006 s and T(60°) = 2.153 s.

Model and numbers

Through variable r(t) = cos ωt, ω = 2πf. Impedance Z = across / through (Rowell, MIT 2.151):

resistance type: e = Z r, Z = R, 1/b, 1/bt   capacitance type: e = (1/C) ∫ r dt = sin ωt / (Cω), C = C, m, J   inductance type: e = L dr/dt = −Lω sin ωt, L = L, 1/k, 1/kt

Power p = e · r (W in every domain shown). Stored energy: ½ C e² for the capacitance type (½ m v² for a mass), ½ L r² for the inductance type (F²/2k for a spring). The resistance type stores nothing; the counter shows the energy it has dissipated as heat.

Lecture 3, Sections 3.5 to 3.7, Eqs. (3.7) to (3.11). The thermal domain is left out on purpose: temperature × heat-flow rate is not a power.

Model and numbers

Machine: node B carries the mass m, the damper b and (if present) the spring k₂ to the ground; a series spring k₁ may sit between the force and node B. m dvB/dt = F − b vB − k₂ xB. The series spring carries F in every case, so it does not change vB; it changes the velocity of the point where F acts: vA = vB + (1/k₁) dF/dt.

Circuit (force-current analogy, Firestone 1933): C deB/dt = i − eB/R − iL2, L₂ diL2/dt = eB, with C = m, R = 1/b, L₂ = 1/k₂. Both are integrated with fourth-order Runge–Kutta from rest.

Check against make_figs_L03.py: Example 1 with m = 1 kg, b = 4 N·s/m, k = 100 N/m and a 10 N step settles at F/b = 2.5 m/s with time constant m/b = 0.25 s. The car (1000 kg, 50 N·s/m, 1250 N) settles at 25 m/s = 90 km/h with time constant 20 s.

Rule. Elements that share a velocity are in parallel: their admittances add. Elements that carry the same force are in series: their impedances add. Element impedances: damper 1/b, mass 1/(ms) to the ground, spring s/k.

  2. After answering, read the step response: does the force point keep moving (a damper path to the ground), settle (a spring path) or oscillate?
